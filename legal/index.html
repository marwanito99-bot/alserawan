Part One

Privacy Policy

Al Serawan Group and its subsidiary Max Worldwide Mastering L.L.C ("MWM", "we", "us", "our") are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

This policy applies to all users of alserawan.com and any registration forms, tracking tools, or contact methods provided through our platform.

Privacy — Section 1

Data We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Full name, company name, job title
  • Email address and phone number
  • Country and city of operation
  • Product descriptions, quantities, and shipping requirements (Sourcing Requests)
  • Company registration details and trade licences (Supplier & Freight registrations)
  • Shipment tracking numbers (Tracking page)
  • Messages submitted via contact and feedback forms

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring URL (how you arrived at our site)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)

Information we do not collect

  • Payment card numbers or banking details
  • Government-issued ID numbers
  • Biometric data
  • Data from minors under 18 years of age
Privacy — Section 2

How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • Processing your requests — Sourcing, freight, and supplier registrations require your data to connect you with the right parties and deliver our services.
  • Shipment tracking — Tracking numbers and associated order data are used to provide real-time shipment status.
  • Communication — We use your contact details to respond to enquiries, send quotations, and provide service updates.
  • Improving our services — Aggregate, anonymised usage data helps us understand how the site is used and improve the user experience.
  • Legal compliance — We may use data to comply with customs authorities, shipping regulations, and applicable law in China and other jurisdictions we operate in.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing without your prior consent.

Privacy — Section 3

Data Sharing

We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

Operational partners

To fulfil your sourcing or shipping request, relevant details (product specifications, quantities, destination) may be shared with verified suppliers, freight partners, or customs agents. We only share what is operationally necessary.

Service providers

We use Google Workspace (Forms, Sheets) to manage form submissions and data storage. These services are governed by Google's privacy policy. No data is shared with any other third-party platform without your knowledge.

Legal requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or governmental authority in China, Syria, or any other jurisdiction where we operate.

Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance of any such change.

Privacy — Section 4

Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses minimal browser storage for functional purposes only:

  • Language preference — We store your selected language (English / Arabic / Chinese) in browser localStorage so it persists across pages. No tracking cookies are set.
  • No advertising cookies — We do not use Google AdSense, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising tracking technologies on this site.
  • No third-party analytics cookies — We do not currently run cookie-based analytics (e.g. Google Analytics with cookies).

You can clear your browser's localStorage at any time through your browser settings. This will reset your language preference to English.

Privacy — Section 5

Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we must fulfil.
  • Objection — Object to processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Data portability — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info.mwm@alserawan.com. We will respond within 14 business days.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations — typically no longer than 5 years from your last interaction with us. Shipment records may be retained longer for customs and regulatory compliance.
